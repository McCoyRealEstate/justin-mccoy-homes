# Data accuracy and compliance requirements

Owner direction, 2026-11-25: prioritize accurate inventory, details, tours, amenities and listings before new features. These are proposed engineering controls, not a representation of Lennar policy or corporate approval.

- Use authorized public Lennar sources for published property facts; track source and last successful verification. Do not expose internal-only data.
- Validate exact homesite identity, community, price, availability, specifications and official listing link. Do not infer sold status from media errors. Distinguish failed refresh from confirmed removal. Flag stale inventory and avoid presenting stale prices as current.
- Verify amenities at community/collection level; distinguish planned from completed amenities. Omit unknown facts rather than infer them.
- Prefer exact homesite photography; label model/reference imagery and approved cross-community media exceptions. Never present reference imagery as the actual home. Waterlin McCartney exception must retain reference disclosure.
- Preserve manually verified tour links; distinguish automated security challenges from confirmed login requirements. Do not expose restricted resources as publicly playable. Verify replacement configuration and public playback.
- Mortgage estimates must disclose assumptions, HOA/CDD treatment and financing limitations. Do not publish unverified incentives, rates or commissions; include applicable dates, eligibility and approved disclosures.
- Validate refresh, data consistency, links, mobile/desktop UI, media and disclosures before feature expansion. Maintain an audit trail and rollback path. Pause new features until outstanding accuracy issues are reviewed.
- Obtain and review current Lennar social media, advertising, brand and data-use policies and any required written approvals before claiming compliance. A disclaimer does not substitute for permission or policy compliance.
